# Limitations and assumptions

**What this experiment is:** a controlled lab-style comparison on PlantVillage. **What it is not:** evidence of field performance.

1. **Dataset realism.** PlantVillage leaves are single leaves photographed on uniform grey/pink backgrounds. Near-perfect closed-set accuracy (98.9–100%) reflects an easy task, not a solved problem. Results say nothing about cluttered field photos, multiple leaves, other lighting or cameras. Generalisation to field photography was not tested and must not be claimed.
2. **Image-level split and possible same-leaf overlap.** PlantVillage has no physical-leaf IDs (only 100 Tomato files carry a leaf number), so a specimen-level split was impossible. Several photos of one leaf may sit in different splits, inflating accuracy and confidence on test. Exact duplicates were removed (13); hashing proves nothing about specimens. Camera frame numbers are dense across splits (most Corn/Grape/Peach test images have a training frame within 5 frames), and adjacency is suggestive but not proof. A retrained frame-block sensitivity split (`final_audit.md`) gave somewhat lower numbers (MobileNet unknown rejection 0.73 → 0.66, AUROC 0.948 → 0.919) with the same qualitative conclusions; the cause cannot be isolated with one seed. Capture-session similarity (shared backgrounds and lighting) is a related shortcut that no split here removes.
3. **Only two unknown species, both from the same family as Tomato.** Potato, Bell pepper and Tomato are all Solanaceae. This may make the open-set task hard in a way that does not generalise to unrelated plants. (Hypothesis, not tested.) The unknown set is also not "unknown" to the pretrained backbone: ImageNet pretraining may have seen related plants.
4. **Softmax is not calibrated.** Maximum probability is a heuristic confidence. MobileNet probabilities saturate near 1.0 (threshold 0.990); HOG+SVM uses Platt-scaled probabilities from a different mechanism. Threshold values and scores are **not directly comparable** across models; only the protocol and the ranking-based AUROC are.
5. **Threshold rule.** The 5th-percentile rule fixes validation acceptance at ~95% by construction; it is not optimised for unknown rejection. A different rule (or a method such as energy/ODIN/Mahalanobis) could do better. The rule was fixed in advance and not tuned on test data.
6. **Small samples, one seed.** 188 known and 300 unknown test images; one split, one training run. Bootstrap CIs resample images as if independent (specimen clustering ignored) and hold the model, threshold and split fixed, so they capture test-sampling noise only, not split/seed/training variance; a percentile bootstrap of an all-correct sample collapses to [1, 1], so exact binomial intervals are also reported (188/188 → 0.981–1.000). Only two unknown species were withheld, so "unknown rejection" is not an estimate for other species. The HOG vs MobileNet closed-set difference (2 errors) is not meaningful.
7. **Pool risk depends on class mix.** Combined risk–coverage uses 188 known vs 300 unknown images; with more unknowns, risk is higher. Absolute pool-risk values are specific to this ratio. The known-only curve is shown separately.
8. **Classical baseline is minimal.** HOG on grayscale with fixed SVM hyper-parameters, no tuning, no colour features. It is a transparent baseline, not the best classical model.
9. **Model scope.** Frozen backbone only; no fine-tuning or architecture comparison was run. Augmentation is mild and was not ablated.
10. **Synthetic stress test.** Brightness and blur applied in memory to copies of known test images only (one blur and one brightness family, no noise, rotation, background or camera changes); it is not evidence about real degradations, and unknown images were not perturbed.
11. **Explanations.** No experiment here shows *which* visual features drive decisions. Statements such as "similar leaf shape" are observations of gallery images, not causal claims.
12. **Reproducibility.** Tested on one machine (Apple M4, MPS): a full retrain reproduced predictions bit-identically. Other hardware may differ slightly. Dataset files were verified byte-identical to upstream commit `7f7ecc7e`. The SVM uses a deprecated `probability=True` path in scikit-learn 1.9 (works; pinned).
13. **Pool risk depends on class mix.** See item 7; the 0.313 MobileNet pool risk assumes 61.5% unknown images.
14. **AUROC and the threshold answer different questions.** AUROC (ranking quality, threshold-free) can be high while a fixed 95%-validation-acceptance threshold still accepts 27% of unknowns. Neither tells how the system behaves on a real, unknown mix of inputs.
15. **Untested:** CUDA/CPU runs, clean-machine reinstall from scratch, real-browser Streamlit upload (upload logic was tested with Streamlit's `AppTest` only), EXIF-rotated and very large uploads.
